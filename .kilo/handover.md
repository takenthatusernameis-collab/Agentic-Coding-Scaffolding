They now operate on the full `parse_qsl` pair list, preserving duplicates and only replacing or removing the targeted key. Added `test_add_param_preserves_duplicate_values`, `test_add_param_replaces_all_occurrences_on_update`, and `test_remove_preserves_duplicate_values_of_other_keys` (fail before, pass after).
